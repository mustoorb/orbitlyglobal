07 Questions

Before you ask.

Ask us directly
What happens in the free audit?

We look at how your business actually runs day to day, then show you where the hours go and which parts are worth automating. You leave with a plain-language picture, whether or not you work with us.

What if automation isn't worth it for us?

Then we'll tell you. Honest scoping is the point of the audit: we'd rather say no than sell you something you don't need.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We explain everything in plain language. If you can't follow it, we haven't explained it well enough.

Can you connect to our scanners, printers or machines?

Yes. Hardware-linked automation is the rarest thing we do: barcode scanners, label printers, sensors, scales and other devices connected to the same system as your orders and stock.

What is orbitly robotics?

Our fourth segment, coming soon: mechatronics and robotic systems that take automation from software into physical movement. The first build is in development. Choose "Robotics" in the form below and we'll tell you when it launches.

Cloud or on our own machines?

Either. We build systems on-cloud or off-cloud, depending on what suits your business, budget and connection.

Who owns the work at the end?

You do. Code, design, accounts and documentation are all handed over. No lock-in, and you can leave any time.
