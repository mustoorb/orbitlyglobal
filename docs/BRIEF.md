## 2. Positioning

### The problem with the old positioning
"Websites + SaaS + automation for all industries" reads as a generalist who takes anything.
Specialists win deals; generalists compete on price. Orbitly keeps all three services but
**leads with one sharp wedge**.

### The wedge
**Orbitly finds the repetitive work draining a business and automates it, then builds the
website and custom systems around it.** Automation is the lead act. SaaS and websites are how
the work gets delivered and shown to the world.

### One-liner
> We automate the boring work out of your business.

### Positioning statement
> For small and medium business owners who lose hours every week to manual admin, spreadsheets
> and tools that don't talk to each other, **Orbitly** is the automation-first studio that
> finds the busywork, automates it in software *and* hardware, and builds the website and
> systems around it. Unlike web agencies that only make pages, or dev shops that only write
> code, Orbitly is measured in **hours handed back**, with one person you can actually call.

### Differentiators (use these, in this order)
1. **Software + hardware automation under one roof.** Almost no small studio offers
   hardware-integrated automation (scanners, sensors, printers, machines). This is the rarest
   thing Orbitly does. Make it visible; don't bury it in a list.
2. **Outcome language, not tech language.** We talk in hours saved and money kept, not stacks.
3. **One point of contact, start to finish.** No account managers, no hand-offs.
4. **You own everything.** Code, design, accounts, documentation: all handed over. No lock-in.
5. **Honest scoping.** Free audit first; if automation isn't worth it, we say so.

### Brand idea
**Momentum, built bit by bit.** An orbit is motion that keeps going without constant pushing.
That's what good automation gives a business. "Bit by bit" is both the motto and the method:
small, visible steps, and a nod to the bits of software.

## 3. Mission, vision, values

**Mission:** Give small businesses back the hours that repetitive work steals, with software
that leaves things a little better than it found them.

**Vision:** A world where running a small business means doing the work you started it for,
not the admin around it.

> Tone note: the mission is about making the world better through software, but must never
> sound like a generic tech manifesto. Keep it concrete and human.

**Values**
| Value | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Honest before clever | We tell clients when they don't need us. No inflated promises. |
| Outcomes over output | Success = hours saved, sales made, errors removed. Not lines of code. |
| Bit by bit | Small steps, shipped often, progress always visible to the client. |
| You own it | Full handover, documentation, no lock-in. |
| Plain language | If the client can't understand it, we haven't explained it well enough. |
